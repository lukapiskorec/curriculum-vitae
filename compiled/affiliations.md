# Affiliations

## {protocell:labs} (since 2021)

Co-founded with Kane Borg in Helsinki in 2021 as a digital laboratory merging artistic and research practice. Within the lab they investigate computational structures, mathematical forms of organization, design algorithms, artificial biology, emergent morphogenesis, generative AI and digital graphics. The preferred mode of expression is chain-agnostic, long-form generative collections, released on the fxhash platform since 2021 and other platforms since. Collections often feature complex 3D geometry, choreographed animation and interactivity.

As a matter of principle, the lab keeps its code open — unminified, unobfuscated, and available on public repositories such as GitHub — and documents its practice through writing and recorded talks. Code for generative collections is stored on-chain (Ethereum, Tezos) or on decentralized protocols (IPFS).

- **Website**: <https://protocell.xyz>
- **GitHub**: <https://github.com/protocell-labs>

## TEN Studio (since 2015)

Co-founded in Zürich in 2015 as TEN Association, an international collective working at the intersection of research and design practice. Now operates as TEN Studio with bases in Zürich and Belgrade. Focus on computational design, digital fabrication, and AI-driven design workflows.

**Recognition:**

- 2018 — Swiss Art Awards, 1st prize in Architecture, for the project *Studiolo* (exhibited at Messe Basel).
- 2024 — Royal Academy Dorfman Prize, shortlisted.

- **Website**: <https://ten.studio>

## Gramazio Kohler Research, ETH Zürich (2011–2017)

Research Assistant at the Chair for Architecture and Digital Fabrication at the Department of Architecture, ETH Zürich. Worked on the development of robotic digital fabrication techniques and their applications in architecture across more than a dozen built projects (see [`built-works.md`](built-works.md)) and four peer-reviewed publications (see [`publications.md`](publications.md)).

From 2011 to 2014, led the Chair's elective course and elective thesis workshops. From 2015 to 2017, co-organized and taught at the MAS ETH in Architecture and Digital Fabrication.

- **Website**: <https://gramaziokohler.arch.ethz.ch/>

## Aalto University — Design of Structures (2017–2024)

University Teacher (2017–2023) and Lecturer (2024) in Design of Structures at the Department of Architecture, School of Arts, Design and Architecture, Aalto University, Helsinki. Conducted courses in algorithmic design and digital storytelling at bachelor, master, and PhD levels.

In 2019, started a PhD at Aalto in generative machine learning for architecture (paused in 2022).
