## Book draft (unpublished)

### Mathematical principles of computational design

- **Status**: Unpublished draft (~500 pages)
- **Started**: 2019, with intensive writing during the COVID pandemic in 2020
- **Topic**: Mathematical principles underlying computational design — formal foundations, algorithmic methods, and the relation between code-driven design and natural processes.

A draft of over 500 pages, still unpublished. Referenced in the 2024 essay *Artist and Computer* (see [`writings.md`](writings.md)) as an ongoing source of inspiration for the author's practice.
