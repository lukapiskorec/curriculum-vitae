# Teaching

Teaching activities at Aalto University (2017–2024) and ETH Zürich (2011–2017), plus standalone workshops and supervised master theses.

---

## Regular university teaching

### 2017–2024 — Aalto University, Helsinki

**Design of Structures group**, Department of Architecture, School of Arts, Design and Architecture.
University Teacher (2017–2023) and Lecturer (2024).

Courses taught:

- **Algorithmic design** — bachelor / master / PhD levels.
- **Digital storytelling** — bachelor / master levels.

Supervision of architecture design studios in addition to coursework.

### 2011–2017 — ETH Zürich

**Chair for Architecture and Digital Fabrication / Gramazio Kohler Research**, Department of Architecture.
Teaching lead.

Courses taught:

- **Digital fabrication**.
- **Architectural design** (incorporating computational and robotic methods).

## Mentored master theses (MAS DFAB, ETH Zürich, 2016)

All theses listed below were submitted for the MAS in Architecture and Digital Fabrication programme. Thesis advisors of record: Prof. Fabio Gramazio and Prof. Matthias Kohler.

- **Spatial Textile Hybrids: Self-Forming 3D-Printing-Induced Structures** — Jorge Christie.
- **Robotic Fabrication of Catalan Vaulting: Controlling Feasibility through Curvature Manipulation** — Ioannis Mirtsopoulos.
- **The Vitruvian Robot: Integrated Analogue and Digital Parameters for Design and Fabrication of Spatial Tubular Extrusion** — Shiu Lun Cheung.
- **Robotic Spatial Bending of Glass Rods** — Wei-Yu Hsiao.
- **Robotic Elastic Bending of Glue Laminated Wood Veneer** — Fabio Scotto.
- **Autonomous 3D Printing of Spatially Extruded Structures through Machine Learning** — Katrin Hochschuh and Jose Paixao.
- **Adaptive Building Skin** — Larisa Gabor and Anastasia Zaytseva.
