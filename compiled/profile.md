# Profile — Luka Piškorec

Computational architect, generative artist, researcher and educator working across computational design, robotic fabrication, and algorithmic art. Co-founder of TEN Studio (Zürich/Belgrade) and {protocell:labs} (Helsinki). Based in Helsinki, Finland.

## At a glance

- Trained as an architect at the University of Zagreb (BSc, 2005–2008) and ETH Zürich (MSc, 2009–2011).
- Six years at Gramazio Kohler Research, ETH Zürich (2011–2017), developing robotic digital fabrication.
- University Teacher (2017–2023) and Lecturer (2024) in Design of Structures, Aalto University, Helsinki.
- PhD in generative machine learning for architecture started at Aalto in 2019, paused in 2022.
- Co-founded TEN Studio (2015) — Swiss Art Awards 2018 (1st prize, Architecture, *Studiolo*); Royal Academy Dorfman Prize shortlist (2024).
- Co-founded {protocell:labs} (2021) — long-form generative collections released on fxhash, Verse, Nifty Gateway, and others since 2021.

## Spelling note

`Piškorec` (with the diacritic on Š) is the canonical spelling. `Piskorec` (simplified, no diacritic) is used on social media handles, email, and some institutional records, and is acceptable in informal or ASCII-only contexts.
