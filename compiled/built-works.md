# Built Works

Physical structures, installations, walls and pavilions built between 2010 and 2018, primarily during the Gramazio Kohler Research period and with TEN Studio. Generative digital works are listed in [`generative-collections.md`](generative-collections.md); other coded works in [`coded-works.md`](coded-works.md); exhibition appearances in [`exhibitions.md`](exhibitions.md).

---

## Studiolo (2018)

- **Year**: 2018
- **Type**: Installation
- **Materials**: Cast Furan sand
- **Built/exhibited at**: Messe Basel, Switzerland
- **Collaborators**: TEN Studio
- **Recognition**: 1st prize, Swiss Art Awards 2018, Architecture category

Installation cast in Furan sand, presented as TEN Studio's entry to the Swiss Art Awards 2018, where it won first prize in the architecture category.

- **Built at**: ETH Zürich

## Iridescence Print (2015)

- **Year**: 2015
- **Type**: Curving wall
- **Materials**: Thermoplastic
- **Process**: Robotic spatial extrusion (additive)
- **Built at**: ETH Zürich
- **Exhibited at**: Palais de Tokyo, Paris, France (2015)
- **Collaborators**: Gramazio Kohler Research
- **Publication**: Helm V., Willmann J., Thoma A., Piškorec L., Hack N., Gramazio F., Kohler M. "Iridescence Print: Robotically Printed Lightweight Mesh Structures." *3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing* 2(3) (2015): 117–122.

A curving wall produced through a robotic process of thermoplastic spatial extrusion, exhibited at Palais de Tokyo.
