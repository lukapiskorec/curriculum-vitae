# Awards and Grants

Awards, prizes, and shortlisting honours, plus research funding received as principal applicant or main researcher.

## Awards and recognitions

### 2024 — Royal Academy Dorfman Prize, shortlisted (TEN Studio)

TEN Studio shortlisted for the Royal Academy Dorfman Prize 2024.
Reference: <https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/article/2024/jul/28/ra-dorfman-prize-2024-shortlist-livyj-bereh-salima-naji-bplus-ten>

### 2018 — Swiss Art Awards, 1st prize, Architecture (TEN Studio)

First prize in the Architecture category at the Swiss Art Awards 2018, for the project *Studiolo*. Exhibited at Messe Basel. With TEN Studio. See [`built-works.md`](built-works.md) and [`exhibitions.md`](exhibitions.md).

### 2016 — Best Workshop Award RobArch 2016, runner-up

Runner-up for the Best Workshop Award at RobArch 2016, Sydney, Australia, for the workshop *Spatially Extruded Structures*. See [`teaching.md`](teaching.md).

### 2012 — 1st prize, International Student Competition Velux 2012 (mentor)

Acted as mentor to the winning student team (Li Bo and Ge Man).
